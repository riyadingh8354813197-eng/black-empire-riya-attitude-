Main Sabse Alag,
Main Sabse Juda

Mein sabse alag mein sabse juda
iss bheed mein alag sa RANG hun
tum kehte mujhko be-lagaam
mein aisi hi hun par kam nahi
ye duniya deti gyaan vyaan sab jaata sar ke paar se
mein sikhti apne aap se mein kismat se bhi kam nahi
kya dega waqt mujhko sabaq mein chalti apne hisaab se
saare jhoote chonchle hai sab kuch bhi yaha pe sach nahi
hai dil mein yakeen kamyabi ki chalte rahenge mere khud se kadam
gar bigda kabhi kuch kahin pe agar toh khud se kuch bhi door nahi
junoon mera guroor mujhe fir khauf kisi ka tod na payega
mujhe itna toh khud pe hota yakeen
koi chhod gaya toh pachtayega
naa naam Mera yun mit paayega
har mod pe waqt ab ye batlaayega
Jo dekh na paya qadar meri
kal naam Mera sau baar dohraayega!
